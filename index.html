Training Offerings

Specialized training programs across refining, petrochemicals, fuels, lubricants, catalysts & analytics. Choose a format or explore any option to learn more.

Flagship Programs

  • Petroleum Refining Technology(2–7 weeks)
  • Analysis of Petroleum & Petroleum Products(2–12 weeks)
  • Crude Assay & Physicochemical Methods(2 weeks)
  • Applications of Fuels & Lubricants in Automotive and Industrial Machinery(2 weeks)
  • Physicochemical Analysis & Lubricant Performance Test Methods(2 weeks)
  • ITEC Training Programmes (MEA, Govt. of India)(2 weeks)

Advanced Programs

  • Advance Training on Petroleum Refining & its Aspects(1 week)
  • Processing & Handling of Heavy Crude Oils(1–2 weeks)
  • Fuel Adulteration & Testing of Petroleum Products(1–2 weeks)
  • Electric Mobility & Advanced Automotive Technology(1–2 weeks)
  • Process Integration (Pinch Analysis) for Energy Conservation(1–2 weeks)
  • Real World Driving Emissions / Vehicular Emission & Control(1–2 weeks)
  • Present & Future Automotive / Flex Fuels(1–2 weeks)
  • Advanced Analytical Training (Chromatography, Mass Spectrometry, X-Ray, Thermal Analysis, Elemental & Microscopic)(1–2 weeks)

Specialized Programs

  • Biomass Utilization into Fuels & Chemicals(1–2 weeks)
  • Solvent Extraction Technology(1 week)
  • Operation & Maintenance of CFR Engines(1–2 weeks)
  • Deposition Rating (Small Scale) & Engine Components(1 week)
  • Bitumen & Bitumen Binders Testing (BIS/IRC/SHRP)(1–2 weeks)
  • Corrosion Control & Material Selection in Refineries(1–2 weeks)
  • Life Cycle Analysis & Carbon Neutrality(1–2 weeks)
